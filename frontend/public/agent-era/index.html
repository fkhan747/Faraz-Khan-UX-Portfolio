The shift: from running the journey to supervising it

The 2023 insight was that lending is assisted: an executive drives, and the customer takes the phone only for the moments the law says must be theirs. Agents don't change that insight. They move the executive one seat up.

2023 · Shipped

The executive runs every step

One screen, one action, 11 steps. The executive types, uploads, verifies, and hands the phone to the customer at consent and signature moments.

  • Speed came from focus: no scrolling, no branching
  • ~60% faster processing, ~40% less training (measured)
  • The customer's moments stayed sacred: photo, consent, e-sign
Concept · Agent Era

Agents run the steps, the executive runs the desk

Filing, verification, and drafting become agent work. The executive supervises a queue: approving what's gated, resolving what's escalated, and stepping in when confidence drops.

  • Every agent action is previewed, gated, or logged
  • Below 70% confidence, the console forces escalation
  • The customer's moments stay exactly where they were: human

What stays human, on purpose

FinVista taught me that the handoff is the story. Agents make that lesson sharper, not obsolete. Three kinds of moments never become agent work:

Customer only

Live photo, KYC and bureau consent, OTP, e-signature. The law gives these to the customer, and the interface keeps them ceremonial: plain question, clear consequence, their own hand.

Executive only

Approving gated actions, resolving escalations, overriding an agent, and every conversation that carries the relationship. Judgment is the job description now.

Never automated

Credit decisions and anything touching a protected attribute. Agents prepare the file; they don't decide who deserves money. That line is drawn in the interface, not the policy PDF.
